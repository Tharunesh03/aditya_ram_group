About Adityaram Group

Adityaram Group is a leading company in South India

Adityaram Group is a leading company in South India that has two decades of track record of customer satisfaction with the completion of privileged projects. The real estate giant has managed to spread its wings over the three South Indian states — Tamil Nadu, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

The Group functions in the real estate sector through its company named Adityaram Properties Private Limited. With its landmark offerings, the company has managed to carve a space for itself in the competitive Chennai real estate market.

The business of success includes Adityaram Properties (P) Ltd, Adityaram Studios (P) Ltd, Adityaram Movies, Adityaram Housing (P) Ltd, Adityaram Infrastructure Projects LLP, and Adityaram Convention and Resorts.
